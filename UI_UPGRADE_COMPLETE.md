## 🎨 Design System

### Color Palette:
- **Primary:** Indigo (#6366f1) to Purple (#a855f7) gradients
- **Success:** Green (#22c55e) to Emerald
- **Warning:** Yellow (#f59e0b) to Amber
- **Danger:** Red (#ef4444)
- **Info:** Blue (#3b82f6) to Cyan

### Typography:
- **Font:** Inter (Google Fonts)
- **Headings:** Font-black (900 weight) with gradient text
- **Body:** Font-medium to font-semibold
- **Small text:** Font-medium with tracking

### Effects:
- **Glass:** backdrop-blur-xl with rgba backgrounds
- **Shadows:** Colored shadows matching gradient themes
- **Borders:** Semi-transparent with hover states
- **Animations:** Smooth transitions (200-500ms)
